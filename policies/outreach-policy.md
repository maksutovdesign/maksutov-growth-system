# Outreach Policy

## Purpose
Generate relevant conversations without spam.

## Eligibility before drafting
A lead must have:
- a real business identity
- a credible design need or trigger
- at least one reliable source
- no suppression flag
- no recent duplicate outreach

## Personalization minimum
Each message must contain at least two prospect-specific elements, for example:
- product/site observation
- recent launch/funding/hiring trigger
- specific design problem or opportunity
- relevant portfolio case
- role-specific reason for contacting this person

Changing only the company name is not personalization.

## Contact cadence
Default:
- initial message
- follow-up 1 after 4–6 business days
- follow-up 2 after 7–10 business days
- then stop for at least 90 days unless there is a new legitimate trigger

Maximum cold touches per prospect: 3 per 90-day cycle.
Maximum cold touches per company: 5 people per 90 days, but normally 1–2 preferred.
Avoid simultaneous multi-channel contact unless owner approves a campaign.

## Daily caps
Initial safe defaults:
- 10 new cold emails/day
- 5 follow-ups/day
- 5 studio outreach messages/day
- 5 social DMs/day

Caps are not targets. Increase only after deliverability and response quality are reviewed.

## Message style
- 60–140 words for cold email by default.
- Specific subject line; no fake RE:/FWD:.
- One clear CTA.
- No exaggerated claims.
- No attachment on first cold email unless necessary.
- Link to the most relevant case, not the whole portfolio when a case is clearly better.
- Match recipient language.

## Suppression
Immediately set `do_not_contact=true` when:
- recipient opts out
- address hard-bounces repeatedly
- legal/privacy request requires suppression
- owner marks as suppressed

Never re-add suppressed contacts through enrichment.
