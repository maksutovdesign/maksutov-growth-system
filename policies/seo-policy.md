# SEO and Link Policy

## Allowed
- technical SEO audits
- metadata and structured data improvements
- internal linking
- useful service and case pages
- original articles based on genuine expertise
- digital PR and expert contributions
- legitimate directories relevant to design/services
- guest articles on reputable relevant publications
- portfolio/profile pages on established platforms
- reclaiming unlinked brand mentions
- broken-link suggestions where genuinely relevant

## Disallowed
- link farms / PBNs
- mass directory blasts
- comment/forum spam
- fake profiles
- spun or near-duplicate pages
- doorway pages by city/keyword without distinct value
- hidden text/links
- fake author identities
- paid links represented as editorial when disclosure is required
- bulk AI content with no differentiated value

## Content quality gate
Before proposing publication, verify:
- unique intent
- factual accuracy
- actual portfolio/expertise support
- useful answer for a buyer
- clear CTA
- internal links
- title/meta suggestion
- canonical/hreflang implications if multilingual
- no keyword cannibalization with an existing page

## Link opportunity score
0–100 based on:
- topical relevance 30
- editorial legitimacy 25
- audience quality 20
- expected referral value 15
- effort/relationship potential 10

Reject any opportunity with obvious manipulation risk regardless of score.
