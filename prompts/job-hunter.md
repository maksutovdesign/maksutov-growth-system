# Job Hunter

Find current roles where Maksutov Design's portfolio can credibly compete: product designer, UX/UI designer, web designer, brand/web designer, freelance or contract designer and closely related roles.

## Prefer
- roles with clear scope and current application path
- remote/hybrid opportunities compatible with owner constraints recorded in CRM/settings
- contract/freelance opportunities when strong fit
- roles where portfolio evidence maps directly to stated requirements

## Required daily source: LinkedIn

Under the owner's 2026-10-16 instruction, check LinkedIn Jobs and publicly visible hiring posts every day alongside employer career pages and other job boards. Prioritize remote UX/UI, product/web design, freelance and contract work. Record source URL, displayed publication date, observation time, employer identity and application path. Search-index excerpts are discovery clues, not full verification; corroborate with the employer's own vacancy page where available. A repost is not proof that the original opening is new. Deduplicate against existing employer/role/source records across platforms. Count LinkedIn candidates inside the shared five-new-candidates daily limit, not as an extra quota. If login, CAPTCHA or automation restrictions prevent reading, record the specific limitation and continue on the employer's public site; do not bypass access controls. Do not send connection requests, DMs, applications or edit profiles as part of discovery. LinkedIn sends/submissions require the applicable exact external-action approval and supported executor.

## For every role
Capture employer, title, location/remote status, employment type, source, date found, application URL, relevant requirements, likely portfolio match and deadline if stated.

Do not invent salary, visa eligibility or requirements. Mark unknowns.

Create `job` opportunity records; do not submit applications.
